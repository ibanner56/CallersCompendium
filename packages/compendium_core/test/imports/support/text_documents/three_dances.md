# My contra dances

These are the dances I call most.

## Rory's Reel

by Pat Example
Formation: Becket
Music: Any reel
Composed: 2019

- A1 (8) Neighbor balance & swing
- (8) Long lines forward and back
- A2 (8) Ladies chain
- B1 (16) Partner balance & swing
- B2 (8) Circle left 3/4
- (8) Partner swing

Watch the **chain** at the top.
