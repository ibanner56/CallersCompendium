# Caller's Compendium Beta Test Charter

This is your field guide for the beta. It is a set of **test charters** — short
missions that point you at a part of the app and ask you to explore it the way you
really would, then tell us what you found. The heart of it is simple: **call a real
dance from Perform mode**, and let everything around that — finding dances, building
a program, switching to your own wording — get a real workout on the way.

You do not have to do all of these, and you do not have to do them in order. Pick
the ones that match how you call, and come back for more when you have time.

## How session-based testing works

Each charter below is a **mission, not a script**. You are not checking boxes
against exact steps; you are using the app toward a real goal and paying attention
to what helps and what gets in the way.

A **session** is one focused block of exploring — say 20–60 minutes, or one real
event. For each session:

1. **Pick a charter** (or bring your own goal).
2. **Explore** toward that goal with the app, using your own dances and devices.
3. **Take rough notes as you go** — what you tried, what surprised you, anything
   that felt slow, confusing, or wrong. A note like "couldn't find where to add an
   alt" is plenty.
4. **Report back** using the channels in the [beta guide](./beta-guide.md#how-to-give-feedback):
   the **Beta check-in** form for how a session went overall, the **Bug report**
   form for anything broken, **General feedback** for awkward moments.

### A charter reads like this

Every charter has the same shape so you know what you are being asked:

- **Explore** — what to do.
- **With** — the parts of the app and the kind of data involved.
- **To discover** — the questions we most want your session to answer.

### Before you start

- **Export a backup first.** Open **Settings → General → Export a backup** and keep
  the file somewhere safe. You can explore freely knowing you can
  **Restore from a backup** at any time.
- **Use real dances you know.** The app is most revealing when the material is
  familiar and the stakes are real.
- **Note your setup** — platform (Linux, macOS, Windows, Android, iOS/iPadOS),
  device, and roughly how big your collection is. It helps us read your findings.

## The charters

### Charter 1 — Call a real dance from Perform mode *(the main event)*

> **Explore** calling a dance start-to-finish from **Perform mode**, as if you were
> on stage.
>
> **With** a dance already in your **Collection**, on the device and in the posture
> you would actually call from (tablet on a stand, phone in hand, laptop on the
> sound table).
>
> **To discover:** Can you read the card at a glance from calling distance? Is the
> type big enough, the contrast strong enough? Does the screen stay awake? Can you
> move to the next and previous figure without looking down or fumbling? If you
> need an on-the-fly change mid-dance, can you make it without losing your place?

This is the charter we care about most. If you only run one session, run this one —
ideally **at a real dance**, but a full living-room rehearsal counts too.

### Charter 2 — Find the dance you want in your Collection

> **Explore** searching and browsing your **Collection** to pull up dances quickly.
>
> **With** a collection of your own dances (import some first if it is empty — see
> Charter 6).
>
> **To discover:** When you search by title, author, formation, or level, do the
> right dances come up? Can you find a dance by the **figures** it contains — for
> example a chain followed by a swing? When you cannot remember a title, can you
> still track a dance down? Does anything about sorting or filtering trip you up?

### Charter 3 — Build a program for a real event

> **Explore** building a **Program** for an actual upcoming (or past) gig, end to
> end.
>
> **With** the **Programs** area: real dances, at least one **alt**, and a
> free-text slot like a break or a waltz. Open the **matrix** when you have a few
> dances in.
>
> **To discover:** Is it clear how to add, reorder, and remove slots? Can you add
> an alternate dance and a free-text slot where you need them? Does the **matrix**
> help you see the shape and variety of the evening — and does it match what you
> know about those dances? Would you trust this program enough to print or send it?

### Charter 4 — Call a whole evening at an actual gig

> **Explore** using the app for a full program at a real dance — from the program
> you built in Charter 3 into **Perform mode**, dance after dance.
>
> **With** a real event, real dancers, real lighting and noise.
>
> **To discover:** Does moving from your program into Perform mode and back feel
> smooth between dances? Does the app keep up with the pace of an evening? Did you
> ever have to apologize to the floor because the app slowed you down? What did you
> reach for that was not there?

This is the highest-value session there is. If you have a gig coming up, this is
your charter — and the **Beta check-in** form afterward is exactly for this.

### Charter 5 — Switch the app into your own words (dialect)

> **Explore** changing role names and move wording with **Dialect**, then calling
> with your terms.
>
> **With** the dialect settings: try the shipped **Larks/Robins** and
> **Leads/Follows** presets, and enter at least one wording of your own.
>
> **To discover:** Is it clear how to choose a preset and how to reword a term? Do
> your terms show up everywhere you expect — in the **Collection**, in a
> **Program**, and in **Perform mode**? If you switch dialects between (or during)
> dances, does everything update the way you would want? Does search still find
> dances no matter which words you use?

### Charter 6 — Import dances, then round-trip a backup

> **Explore** bringing dances in from a source, then exporting and restoring a
> backup to make sure nothing is lost.
>
> **With** the **Import** flow (The Caller's Box, ContraDB, Caller's Companion, or
> a Caller's Compendium file) and **Settings → General**'s **Export a backup** /
> **Restore from a backup**.
>
> **To discover:** During import, is the review-and-commit queue clear about what
> will be added before you commit? Do the imported dances look right — figures,
> author, formation? Then: export a backup, and restore it (onto a second device if
> you have one, or after noting what you have). Does everything come back intact —
> dances, programs, custom fields, dialects? A clean round-trip is what makes the
> app trustworthy, so tell us about any gap.
>
> If a dance imports **wrong**, that is exactly what the **Import source problem**
> form is for — include the source or ID.

## What to send us

After a session, a quick note beats a perfect report. The most useful things to
include:

- **Which charter** you ran (or what you were trying to do).
- **Your setup** — platform, device, rough collection size.
- **What worked well** — genuinely, this shapes what we protect.
- **What got in your way** — confusing, slow, missing, or broken. One sentence each
  is fine.
- **Anything that broke** — with steps if you can repeat it, and the dance's source
  if it is notation-related.

Send it through the [feedback channels](./beta-guide.md#how-to-give-feedback): the
**Beta check-in** form for the session overall, **Bug report** for defects,
**General feedback** for rough edges, or a
[Discussion](https://github.com/ibanner56/CallersCompendium/discussions) if you
just want to talk it through.
