Caller's Compendium — User Guide

Welcome! This is the home for Caller's Compendium user documentation — the guides that help you use the app, whether you are cataloguing dances, building a program for Saturday's gig, or calling from the stage.

Caller's Compendium is a free, open-source dance organizer for contra-dance callers, built for desktop, tablet, and phone. It keeps your collection of dances, helps you assemble programs (set lists) for events, and gives you a large-print, high-contrast Perform mode for calling live. It is local-first: your data lives on your own device, the app works fully offline, and there is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. You bring dances in from community sources or your own files, put everything in your own words with a dialect, and take it all with you with a single backup file.

These pages are written for callers and dancers. If you are a developer or you want the design rationale behind a feature, head to the design docs instead — see Looking deeper at the bottom of this page.

New here? Read these three

You can be calling from the app in an evening. In order:

  1. Installation — get the app onto your computer, tablet, or phone.
  2. Getting started — a guided first hour: fill your collection, build a program, call a dance.
  3. Dialect — put the app into the words you already use, so everything you read afterwards reads the way you speak.

Everything else can wait until you need it.

Reading these guides inside the app

Every guide on this page also ships inside Caller's Compendium, so you have it on stage with no signal. Open Guide — it sits beside Collection, Programs, and Settings in the app's main navigation, and also has a link at Settings ▸ About ▸ User guide.

The in-app copy is the same text you are reading now, with two differences:

  • It is text only. Any illustration appears as its description in italics rather than a picture.
  • Links between guides open in the panel, and Back returns you to where you were. Links out to the web open in your browser — nothing is fetched unless you choose to open it.

The app has no search inside the guide, so this page — and the I want to… table above — is the fastest way in. The app's own search box (and the Ctrl-K / Cmd-K shortcut) searches your dances and programs, not the guide.
