## Why this exists

The AGPL (like the GPL) forbids a distributor from imposing restrictions beyond
those in the License. Some application marketplaces — most notably Apple's App
Store — require apps to be delivered under Usage Rules that count as exactly such
additional restrictions, which is why unmodified GPL/AGPL software cannot simply
be uploaded to them. This additional permission resolves that conflict for
Caller's Compendium, so callers can install the app the normal way on their
phones and tablets without weakening the copyleft protections on its source.
