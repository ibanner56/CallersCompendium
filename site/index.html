What it does

Collection

Catalog dance transcriptions with structured, searchable figures. Search by title, author, type, formation, level — even “chain then swing in B2” — plus your own custom fields.

Programs

Create, edit, duplicate, and print or email set lists, with alternates, free-text slots, and a programming matrix computed from the choreography itself. Build from a title list or straight from a ContraDB event.

Performance mode

A large-print, high-contrast, stage-ready calling view with wake-lock, program navigation, on-the-fly adjustments, and screen-reader-friendly figure rendering.

Dialect

Your terms, your phrasing. Role names, move and dancer-term substitutions, and discouraged-term flags are editable presentation settings over a canonical vocabulary — so search always works and your data stays portable.

Imports

Bring dances in from The Caller's Box and ContraDB by link or id, and migrate from Caller's Companion — including its binary .USR library — all through a review-and-commit queue.

Local-first & cross-platform

Everything is stored on your device and the app is fully usable offline. No accounts, and it never collects personal data. One codebase across Linux, macOS, Windows, Android, and iOS/iPadOS — with the interface available in English, German, French, Japanese, Danish, and Dutch.

Join the beta program

We're looking for real callers to put the app through real gigs. Testing is informal: install a build, use it for your calling, and tell us how it held up — what worked, what got in the way, what's missing.

  • No account or sign-in — just grab a build and go.
  • Local-first & private: the app never collects personal data. A check-in is only ever what you choose to share.
  • New? Sign up once so we know you're testing and can line up the right builds.
  • Already testing? Periodic check-ins are structured through a short GitHub form so feedback is easy to triage.
  • On iOS/iPadOS? Signup is how we line up your TestFlight invite.

How it works

  1. New tester? Click Join the beta and fill in the short signup form.
  2. Grab a build from Downloads and use it for your calling.
  3. Every so often, click Send a check-in to tell us how it held up.
  4. Submit. It lands in triage and helps shape the next build.

Prefer to share privately? Email compendium@contra.dance — please leave out personal or identifying data.
